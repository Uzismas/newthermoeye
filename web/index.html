Retinal vessel analysis for OCTA research

Eyezheimer turns an OCTA scan into a reviewable vessel map and measurements, with every limit stated. It is the groundwork for studying retinal biomarkers of Alzheimer's disease — it does not detect the disease today.

How it works

A short, auditable pipeline that runs on the local machine.

  1. UploadResearcher uploads an OCTA en-face image. Size, format and basic modality checks run first.
  2. Quality gateImages that fail checks are blocked with a reason instead of being analysed.
  3. Vessel segmentationA U-Net (ONNX) outlines retinal vessels and reports vessel density with units and provenance.
  4. Human reviewA reviewer records a decision; the research PDF unlocks only after approval.

Roadmap

What must happen before any disease-related claim can be made.

Now: segmentation research

  • Reproducible vessel-segmentation baseline
  • Quality gating and provenance on every result
  • Reviewer-approved research reports

Next: disease research (not started)

  • Ethics approval and an authorised, labelled patient cohort
  • Patient-level train/test split and a pre-defined endpoint
  • Report sensitivity, specificity and AUC honestly — including negative results

Data & privacy

  • Analysis runs locally; images are not sent to external AI services by default.
  • Demonstrations use public or synthetic images only — no patient data.
  • The demo sign-in is a prototype mechanism, not production patient-data security.
